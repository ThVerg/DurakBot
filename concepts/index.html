Durak: four design concepts

The same moment in each: a 5-player game where you are defending against an 8♦. Open the pages on both desktop and phone. Hover and click your playable cards; most concepts animate them onto the table.

Concept A

Arcade

Bright, juicy mobile-game style: teal felt, chunky glossy buttons, character avatars with level badges, big-index cards, playful motion.

Concept B

Atelier

Elegant flat minimal, Apple-style: warm paper canvas, no felt, one cobalt accent, refined typography, a card tracker and win-chance panel. Has a dark mode too.

Concept C

Dacha

Warm illustrated Russian folk-art world: sunlit embroidered tablecloth, samovar and tea, Gzhel card backs, woodcut-style «ДУРАК» wordmark, illustrated characters.

Tabletop
