# DurakBot art assets — licences & attribution

Generated by `web/tools/art/build_all.py`. See `manifest.json` for a machine-readable copy.

## Playing-card faces (`cards/`, `cards4/`, `cards_sm/`), suit emblems (`ui/suit_*.svg`), suit marks on chips

Based on the **English pattern SVG playing cards by Dmitry Fomin**, Wikimedia Commons
(<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Dmitry_Fomin>), released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
The licence was checked for every one of the 36 source files through the Commons API on 2026-10-14.
Each file's URL and SHA-256 hash are pinned in `web/tools/art/sources.json`.

What changed: recoloured palette, new serif indices, re-layout on 2.5:3.5 stock with a procedural
paper texture, enlarged ace pips, an original ornamental ace of spades, and the 4-colour variant.
CC0 doesn't require attribution. We credit Dmitry Fomin as a courtesy.

## Card backs, table materials, avatars, environment, logo, chips

These are original procedural artwork from this repository (`web/tools/art/*.py`), under the
same terms as the DurakBot project.

## Fonts used in artwork

- **EB Garamond** (Georg Duffner, Octavio Pardo): SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for the monogram glyph
  outlines in `ui/logo.svg` / `ui/favicon.svg` and the rasterised lettering on the table logo, ace of spades
  and navy back.
- **P052** (URW++, base35): AGPL-3.0 with font exception. Used only for rasterised card indices.

The Dacha kit (`dacha/fonts/`) ships **Playfair Display** and **Lato** as WOFF2 files under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1; their licence texts are included next to the font files. No other font
files are distributed with the assets.
